# K I B I R A — Project Brief

## Purpose

Create a single creative-studio website under the public-facing name **K I B I R A**. The site will bring together two distinct brands while preserving their individual identities:

- **Gevurah Pictures** — photography portfolio, services, and bookings.
- **The Rich Art Guy** — digital-art collections, commissions, and future shop.

The site will later support a separate **K I B I R A Foundation** area for art, technology, and youth empowerment. The foundation must remain visually and operationally distinct from the commercial creative brands.

## Product direction

- Start with a public-facing creative website.
- Keep one shared system behind the scenes for media, enquiries, and future social publishing.
- Later add a private creator dashboard for uploading, organizing content, preparing platform-specific posts, and securely connecting social accounts.
- Do not put passwords or social-media secrets in this project.

## Brand direction

### K I B I R A

Use spaced lettering in public-facing design. Use `kibira` without spaces for domains, handles, URLs, code, and email addresses.

### Gevurah Pictures

Premium, intimate, editorial photography. The visual language is restrained and monochrome, with strong portraiture, generous whitespace, and carefully composed typography.

### The Rich Art Guy

Expressive digital art with a vivid, narrative, culturally textured gallery feel. The visual language should support colour, collections, artwork detail, and storytelling.
